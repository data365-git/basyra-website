# Design direction — locked from the reference's real DNA

## Taste anchor (extracted live from humoyunsobirov.tilda.ws/mentorlik5 DOM)
- **Serif display, heavy use** — Times-style serif used 800+ times → big elegant serif headlines.
- **Black-dominant** — ~20 black background sections. It is a DARK site, not grayscale.
- **Warm gold/cream accents** — `rgb(255,222,161)` cream-gold, `rgb(225,196,142)` tan.
- **Punchy color-block pops** — coral `rgb(249,93,81)`, bright yellow `rgb(255,247,5)`, green `rgb(98,197,132)` used as full bg blocks.
- Body: Arial / SF Pro (clean grotesk). High contrast black/white flips.

## What this means (why my earlier concepts felt "template/cold")
- I used teal + grotesk (Bricolage) + instrument aesthetic = cold, designery, NOT the user's taste.
- The user's taste = **warm editorial-on-black: large elegant serif + gold + bold color pops + photography**.

## NEW direction — "NOIR EDITORIAL" (beat the reference at its own game)
- **Canvas:** true near-black `#0B0B0C`, with one parchment light cut-in for rhythm.
- **Display:** Instrument Serif (huge, high-contrast, elegant, italic accents) — more premium/cool than Times.
- **Body:** Inter. **Labels:** mono.
- **Accent:** warm gold `#E7C49A` as the through-line; ONE punchy **coral `#FF6A45`** statement block (numbers/CTA) for energy — echoes the reference's color pops.
- **Photography-forward:** intentional photo frames (warm duotone + grain + caption) where the client drops real photos of Abdulboriy / classroom / students. Placeholders must read as deliberate art-direction, not gray boxes.
- **Big numbers, bold editorial layout, full-bleed color sections, refined motion.**
- Content = same real Uzbek copy from TZ (Business Navigator, 2.5mo offline, cases, etc.).
