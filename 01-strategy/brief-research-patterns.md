## ROLE: UX RESEARCHER / STRATEGIST — DERIVED BRIEF

**Who the mentor is.** Abdulboriy Abduqodirov is an Uzbek sales-systems operator, not a motivational coach. He has systematized 10+ real businesses (Cambridge, Najot Ta'lim, K-Davo, Ucan Consulting, Colba, Zin-Nur, Rida, Basyra, edfix, Jahon School), trained 80+ entrepreneurs / ROPs / top-managers, and launched 40+ outsource sales departments across 7 industries (auto, retail, tourism, logistics, B2B agency, IT startup, private school, training center). His authored program is **Business Navigator** — a 2.5-month *offline* mentorship that turns chaotic sales processes into a numbered, KPI-driven system. His differentiator is the word he repeats: **tizim** (system) and **raqam** (numbers) — sales grow through *process optimization*, not bigger ad budgets or more headcount.

**Who the audience is.** Two segments, addressed together: (1) **Tadbirkorlar** — founders doing $X but stuck managing people by feel, burning marketing budget, with a sales team whose "eyes aren't burning"; (2) **Top-menejerlar / ROPs** — operators who want to control sellers by numbers instead of being the bottleneck themselves. Both are post-startup, revenue-generating, time-poor, and skeptical of "infobiznes" fluff. They respond to proof, numbers, and named cases — not hype.

**What action the page must drive.** A single primary conversion: **Ro'yxatdan o'tish / apply to the next cohort** (offline, seats limited). Secondary: a low-friction "diagnostika" / consultation request for the unsure. Every section ladders toward the application form.

**Emotional tone that earns trust here.** Calm operator authority. Concrete over aspirational. The reference (Humoyun Sobirov) leans loud infobiznes-flex; we win by reading as *the systems guy the flashy guys hire* — measured, numerical, premium, credible. Trust is built with named cases, hard counts, and a transparent program structure, not countdown timers and shouting.

**One-paragraph brief.** *Business Navigator is a 2.5-month offline mentorship by Abdulboriy Abduqodirov that teaches Uzbek entrepreneurs and sales leaders to grow revenue through systems and numbers — not bigger budgets. The homepage must convert skeptical, time-poor operators into cohort applicants by proving, fast, that this is an operator's system (10+ businesses, 80+ graduates, 40+ sales-dept launches, real named cases) and that the program hands them a concrete map: KPI design, process digitization, change-management, growth-point diagnosis, and an AI curator (Basyra AI). It must read more premium and more credible than the loud reference it competes with.*

## ROLE: CREATIVE DIRECTOR — QUALITY BAR & DIRECTION SIGN-OFF

The reference is *content-rich but visually generic Tilda* — strong proof, weak art direction (grayscale default blocks, avatar walls, inconsistent rhythm). That's the gap. We don't copy its layout; we beat its **finish**: a real type scale, deliberate spacing rhythm, accessible contrast, intentional photography placeholders, and one signature motion idea per direction.

**Three deliberately divergent directions, signed off:**

- **V1 — "The Operating System"** · Editorial / minimal-premium. Near-monochrome, typography-led, dense data presented calmly. For the buyer who trusts restraint = competence. *This is the safe, premium flagship.*
- **V2 — "Numbers Go Up"** · Bold / high-energy. Dark canvas, electric accent, oversized numerals, animated metrics, momentum. For the ambitious operator who wants to feel the upside. *This is the loud, modern challenger to the reference — beating it at its own game.*
- **V3 — "Abdulboriy aka"** · Warm / personal / story-led. Photography-forward, first-person voice, paper-warm palette, narrative scroll. Trust through the human. *This is the relationship play — closest to how mentorship actually sells in Uzbek culture.*

All three share the **same copy and same proof** so the client compares *art direction*, not content.

## ROLE: UX + UI — PHASE 2 PATTERN-LIBRARY SYNTHESIS

### Recurring section structure (the spine all 3 use)
`Hero (promise + proof chip + CTA)` → `Trust bar (counts + logos)` → `Problem (Dasturdan avval)` → `Transformation (Dasturdan keyin)` → `The system / 3 skills` → `Offer: what's inside Business Navigator` → `Proof: named cases + graduates + industries` → `Mentor (Abdulboriy)` → `Format & details` → `FAQ` → `Final CTA / application`. This mirrors the reference's logic but tightens it and fixes its rhythm.

### 4 strongest hero patterns observed (we use a different one per variation)
1. **Statement-headline + proof-chips** (Justin Welsh, Dan Martell) → V1. Calm, type-first, numbers as quiet authority.
2. **Oversized animated metric** (Linear, Scott Galloway energy) → V2. The number *is* the hero.
3. **Portrait + first-person promise** (Marie Forleo, Ali Abdaal) → V3. Face builds trust instantly.
4. **Split outcome (before→after)** — strong but we demote it to a dedicated section, not the hero, to avoid the reference's "wall of blocks" feel.

### Type / color / spacing systems that recur in the best examples
- **Type:** one display face + one workhorse grotesk. Big jump ratio (display ~3–5rem, body 1.0625–1.25rem), generous line-height (1.5–1.7 body), tight display tracking. Fluid `clamp()` scales (utopia.fyi).
- **Color:** restrained base (near-black / paper) + ONE confident accent. Premium sites almost never use >1 saturated hue. Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 body, ≥ 3:1 large.
- **Spacing:** consistent 8px rhythm, section padding `clamp(64px,10vw,140px)`, max content measure ~68ch for text, ~1200px shell.
- **Motion:** ONE signature idea per site + universal fade/translate-on-scroll at `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Conversion devices that build trust (pulled from research, mapped to our proof)
- **Hard counts** → "10+ tizimlashtirilgan biznes · 80+ bitiruvchi · 40+ sotuv bo'limi zapuski · 7 yo'nalish."
- **Named cases (not anonymous avatars)** → Cambridge, Najot Ta'lim, K-Davo, Ucan, Basyra, edfix… (the reference's weakness is anonymity; named logos beat it).
- **Specific outcome framing** → before/after lifted verbatim from the TZ (real pain language).
- **Transparent structure** → 13 lessons, weekly cadence, 2 speakers, tadbiq manager, AI curator → removes "what do I actually get" doubt.
- **Risk reversal / qualification** → cohort is offline + limited seats + application = scarcity *and* selectivity (premium signal).
- **Single dominant CTA** repeated at hero, after offer, after proof, and in a sticky bar.
