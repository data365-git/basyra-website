# Design Research Dossier — Business Navigator (Personal Brand)

**Brand:** Business Navigator by Abdulboriy Abduqodirov — Uzbek sales-mentor PERSONAL website.
**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Scope:** PERSONAL websites only — coaches, mentors, experts, authors, speakers, and individual designer portfolios. Strong Russia/CIS subset included. Corporate/SaaS sites are intentionally excluded; a personal brand has design freedom those don't.
**Why this dossier:** Prior drafts felt "like templates." This isolates the art-direction moves — portrait/photography treatment, expressive type, color, motion, storytelling scroll — that make a *personal* site feel bespoke and premium.

> Data note: Fonts/palettes are extracted live from the DOM (computed styles, rgb→hex). Hero / visual-signature / takeaway lines are the researcher's read of each loaded page.
> Screenshots: the connected Chrome could not capture via CDP (screenshot endpoint errored on every attempt), so this dossier relies on DOM-extracted design tokens plus rich written visual descriptions. Noted honestly per the brief.

## SITE ANALYSIS — PERSONAL SITES

### 1. Justin Welsh — justinwelsh.me  `[personal / solopreneur mentor]`
- Fonts: Display: `Playfair Display` / Body: `Lora`
- Palette: `#0f0e0c` warm near-black bg · `#f2ede5` cream text · `#857f77` taupe · `#1a1714` panel
- Hero pattern: Warm near-black background, large 56px serif headline in cream, single-column editorial, very restrained.
- Visual signature: Serif-on-serif (Playfair + Lora) on a *warm* black (not cold #000) — reads like a personal letterpress book, intimate and authored.
- Takeaway: Warm near-black + cream + serif = instant "trusted personal mentor." A direct reference for Business Navigator's most premium direction.

### 2. Dennis Snellenberg — dennissnellenberg.com  `[personal / designer portfolio, Awwwards SOTY]`
- Fonts: Display/Body: `Dennis Sans` (bespoke grotesk), weight 450
- Palette: `#1c1d20` charcoal bg · `#ffffff` · `#455ce9` electric blue accent · `#141517` deep panel
- Hero pattern: His NAME set at a colossal 252px filling the viewport, charcoal canvas, a single blue link accent, cursor-following circular hover labels.
- Visual signature: Name-as-hero at extreme scale + a custom-named typeface ("Dennis Sans") — the personality IS the type. Smooth Locomotive scroll, magnetic buttons, page transitions.
- Takeaway: A personal site can lead with the person's NAME at heroic scale. One accent color, charcoal ground, a custom-feeling sans = confident and unmistakably his.

### 3. Robin Noguier — robin-noguier.com  `[personal / interactive designer portfolio]`
- Fonts: Display: `Eksell Display` (characterful serif) / Body: `Silka`
- Palette: `#0d0d0d` near-black bg · `#ffffff` · dusty teal `#3d6681` · `#d5dedd` mist
- Hero pattern: Near-black canvas, expressive 157px display serif, full-screen project imagery that reveals on scroll with WebGL hover.
- Visual signature: A *quirky display serif* (Eksell, with playful ball terminals) paired with a clean grotesk body — personality from the typeface choice, not decoration. Muted teal as the only color.
- Takeaway: One distinctive display serif gives a personal site instant character. Pair it with a neutral grotesk so it stays legible and adult.

### 4. Brittany Chiang — brittanychiang.com  `[personal / engineer portfolio]`
- Fonts: Display/Body: `Inter` (700 for name)
- Palette: `#0f172a` slate-navy bg · `#e2e8f0` light slate text · `#94a3b8` muted · teal accent `#5eead4` / `#2dd4bf`
- Hero pattern: Two-column — left half is a STICKY intro (name, role, nav) that stays fixed; right half scrolls through work. 48px name.
- Visual signature: The sticky split layout: identity pinned left, content streaming right. Slate-navy + single teal accent + spotlight cursor glow.
- Takeaway: A sticky-left identity panel keeps the *person* present the whole scroll — ideal for a mentor whose face/credibility should never leave the screen.

### 5. Aristide Benoist — aristidebenoist.com  `[personal / independent developer portfolio]`
- Fonts: Display: `JWS` + `TNY` (custom) · `Times` accent
- Palette: `#1e1e1e` dark ground · sage `#bac4b8` · gold `#cc9933` · cream `#fff1ce` · pink pop `#ff71cb`
- Hero pattern: Cinematic WebGL transitions between full-bleed project worlds, each with its own color world emerging from a dark base.
- Visual signature: Per-section color "worlds" (sage, gold, cream, pink) blooming out of near-black — color used as narrative chapters, fluid shader transitions.
- Takeaway: A personal site can shift palette per section to tell a story (origin → method → results). Anchor on dark, let each chapter own a warm hue.

### 6. Rauno Freiberg — rauno.me  `[personal / interaction designer]`
- Fonts: Display/Body: custom minimal sans (`X`) · `Times` accent
- Palette: `#ededed` warm-light grey bg · `#171717` near-black text · `#6f6f6f` grey · pops of `#ffff02` / `#030063`
- Hero pattern: A single quiet sentence intro at modest 32px on light grey — almost a document, with exquisitely crafted micro-interactions.
- Visual signature: Extreme restraint + obsessive micro-detail (custom focus rings, springy toggles, pixel-perfect spacing). The polish IS the personality.
- Takeaway: Less can read as more expensive IF every micro-interaction is flawless. For a mentor, this says "I sweat the details."

### 7. Paco Coursey — paco.me  `[personal / engineer-designer]`
- Fonts: Display: `Söhne` / Body: `Inter` · `Newsreader` (serif accent)
- Palette: `#1a1a1a` near-black bg · `#f2f2f2` off-white · `#a0a0a0` grey · `#707070`
- Hero pattern: A quiet single-column "now/writing" index on dark, modest type, lots of breathing room.
- Visual signature: Söhne (Swiss grotesk) for UI + Newsreader serif for prose — a sans/serif voice split that signals "designer who also writes." Subtle command-menu polish.
- Takeaway: Mix a Swiss grotesk (authority) with a literary serif (Newsreader) for long-form. Useful for a mentor who publishes essays/lessons.

### 8. Emil Kowalski — emilkowal.ski  `[personal / designer-educator, "Animations on the Web"]`
- Fonts: Display/Body: refined sans · `Times` accent
- Palette: warm off-white `#fdfdfc` bg · warm near-black `#21201c` text · `#63635e` warm grey · `#f9f9f8`
- Hero pattern: Bright, warm-white reading surface, generous line-height, course/essay index — feels like a beautifully set book page.
- Visual signature: An entirely *warm* greyscale (off-white → warm ink, no pure #fff/#000) that makes a light theme feel soft and human, not clinical.
- Takeaway: For a LIGHT direction, shift every neutral warm (#fdfdfc / #21201c). Warm light = approachable mentor, not sterile SaaS.

### 9. Bruno Simon — bruno-simon.com  `[personal / creative developer portfolio]`
- Fonts: Display: `Amatic SC` (hand-drawn) + `Pally` / Body: `Nunito`
- Palette: `#000000` / `#141414` dark ground · playful pinks `#ffceca` / `#ff87a2` · red pop `#ff0000`
- Hero pattern: A fully drivable 3D toy-car world — you steer a car around his projects. Pure WebGL playground.
- Visual signature: Turns the portfolio into a game; soft hand-drawn display font (Amatic) signals warmth/play against a techy 3D scene.
- Takeaway: Probably too playful for a sales mentor, but the lesson holds: ONE memorable interactive idea makes a site unforgettable. (Use a restrained version.)

### 10. Marie Forleo — marieforleo.com  `[★ coach-mentor / NYT author, B-School — direct analog]`
- Fonts: Display: `IvyPresto Display` (high-contrast serif) / Body: `Area Normal` (geometric sans)
- Palette: `#f6f1eb` warm cream bg · `#1d1b1c` warm ink · terracotta `#c78067` · plum `#764e4e` · lime pop `#e4f58e`
- Hero pattern: Huge 120px elegant serif headline over a confident founder portrait, warm cream ground, generous editorial spacing.
- Visual signature: High-contrast display serif (IvyPresto) for warmth + authority, big founder photography, a warm-neutral palette with ONE unexpected pop (lime). Premium-feminine but ungendered enough to adapt.
- Takeaway: This is the template-killer reference for Business Navigator: editorial serif + warm cream + big personal portrait + a single surprising accent. Steal the structure.

### 11. Lewis Howes — lewishowes.com  `[coach-mentor / podcaster, author]`
- Fonts: Display: `Museo Sans` (700) + script accent `Bethany Elingston` / Body: Museo Sans
- Palette: `#ffffff` / `#f8f7f6` warm white · `#000000` · energetic orange `#f15a23` · `#777777`
- Hero pattern: 124px bold sans headline ("Become the Hero…") + big confident portrait, warm-white ground, orange CTA energy.
- Visual signature: A hand-script accent word dropped into a bold-sans headline adds human warmth; single high-energy orange drives all CTAs.
- Takeaway: A *script accent* on one word humanizes a bold headline. One energetic accent color = motivational without being loud everywhere.

### 12. Ali Abdaal — aliabdaal.com  `[coach-mentor / productivity, author]`
- Fonts: Display: `Recoleta` (friendly rounded serif) / Body: `Elza` (warm grotesk)
- Palette: warm cream `#f9f6f3` / `#ece5e1` bg · warm ink `#1b1624` · sky `#5dcdf1` · yellow `#fdd46b` · brick `#c02b0a`
- Hero pattern: Big friendly portrait + Recoleta headline on warm cream, cheerful multi-accent palette, rounded cards.
- Visual signature: Recoleta's soft rounded serifs + a playful but controlled secondary palette (sky/yellow) = "approachable expert" — smart but never intimidating.
- Takeaway: Recoleta is the friendliest credible serif. Pair with warm cream + 2 cheerful accents for a "this mentor is human and fun" read.

### 13. Sahil Bloom — sahilbloom.com  `[coach-mentor / writer, investor]`
- Fonts: Display: `Bely Display` (elegant serif) / Body: `Brooklyn` sans
- Palette: deep navy `#151b26` / `#323847` · `#ffffff` · confident blue `#358dff`
- Hero pattern: His NAME in 151px Bely serif, navy ground, big portrait, blue accent — authority-forward.
- Visual signature: Name-as-hero in a refined serif over deep navy — "established author" gravity. The serif keeps the blue from feeling corporate.
- Takeaway: If the brand needs blue/navy authority, anchor it with a literary serif name-hero so it reads "thought leader," not "fintech."

### 14. Tim Ferriss — tim.blog  `[coach-mentor / author, podcaster]`
- Fonts: Display/Body: `DM Sans` · `Times` accent
- Palette: warm off-white `#fbf9f9` / `#f6f1f1` · deep ink `#001523` · classic blue `#0071c0`
- Hero pattern: Content-first editorial blog, warm-white reading column, restrained blue links — built for trust and reading volume.
- Visual signature: Almost anti-design restraint — the *credibility of the writing* is the brand; warm whites keep a text-heavy site inviting.
- Takeaway: For a mentor who teaches via long content, a warm-white reading-first layout signals substance over hype.

### 15. James Clear — jamesclear.com  `[coach-mentor / Atomic Habits author]`
- Fonts: Display: `Minion Pro` (classic book serif) / Body: `Europa` (humanist sans)
- Palette: cream `#f9f8f4` bg · near-black `#111111` · warm gold `#f8cd5f` · soft peach `#fde8e2`
- Hero pattern: A book-like cream page, classic serif headings, single-column reading, gold accent on key CTAs.
- Visual signature: Minion Pro (the typeface of literary books) instantly signals "author/teacher." Cream + gold = quiet prestige, zero hype.
- Takeaway: A traditional book serif (Minion/Source Serif) + cream + gold = "this person wrote the book on it." Powerful for a sales *authority*.

### 16. Dan Martell — danmartell.com  `[coach-mentor / coach for founders, author "Buy Back Your Time"]`
- Fonts: Display: `Bebas Neue` (condensed caps) / Body: `Poppins`
- Palette: `#000000` / `#171717` black ground · `#ffffff` · bright cyan `#00beff`
- Hero pattern: 75px condensed all-caps headline on black, big portrait, cyan accent CTAs — punchy, high-energy.
- Visual signature: Bebas Neue condensed caps = sales-energy/urgency; black + one electric cyan keeps it modern, not infomercial.
- Takeaway: A condensed display face delivers "sales momentum." For Business Navigator's bold direction, condensed caps + black + one electric accent could fit a *sales* mentor well — but use sparingly so it stays premium.
