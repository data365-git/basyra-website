# Design PRD — Business Navigator (personal mentorship website)
### For an AI design / image-generation tool. Focus: VISUAL STYLE. Output: 3 distinct homepage design variations.

---

## 1. What to generate
A **homepage UI design** (desktop + mobile) for the personal website of **Abdulboriy Abduqodirov**, an Uzbek sales-systems mentor. Generate **3 distinct visual style variations** (A, B, C below). Each should be a full, polished, high-fidelity landing-page mockup — hero through footer — that looks like a real premium personal-brand site (think Marie Forleo, Dan Martell, James Clear quality), NOT a generic template.

## 2. Context (so the mockup feels real)
- **Brand:** Business Navigator — *Abdulboriy Abduqodirov mentorlik dasturi* · powered by Basyra
- **Product:** a 2.5-month **offline** mentorship that teaches entrepreneurs & sales leaders to grow sales through **systems & numbers**, not bigger budgets.
- **Audience:** (1) Tadbirkorlar (founders managing staff), (2) Top-menejerlar / ROP (sales leaders). Time-poor, skeptical, results-driven.
- **Primary goal of page:** get the visitor to **apply to the next cohort** ("Dasturga yoziling").
- **Language of all UI text:** **Uzbek (Latin)**.
- **Tone:** confident operator authority. Premium, numerical, trustworthy — never hypey/infobiznes-loud.

## 3. Imagery direction (THE most important visual element)
- Lead with **real professional photography of a confident Central-Asian / Uzbek man, ~35–45**, business-casual (shirt or knit, no loud suit), warm and approachable but authoritative.
- Hero = a **large, cinematic photo of the mentor** (upper body, real environment — office, classroom, or studio). Photography is what separates premium from template — make it central, not a tiny avatar.
- Secondary photos: a clean **headshot** (mentor section), a **teaching/workshop scene** (program section), optional **audience/student** shots.
- Treatment per variation (warm-dark cinematic / dynamic lifestyle / bright natural) — see each style.

## 4. Page sections (same content, all 3 variations)
1. **Sticky nav** — logo "Business Navigator", links (Kimga · Tizim · Dastur · Mentor · Keyslar), CTA button **"Dasturga yoziling →"**
2. **Hero** — headline **"Sotuvlaringizni byudjet emas — *tizim* oshiradi."**, subline, CTA "Dasturga yoziling" + "Bepul diagnostika", a ★★★★★ "4.9 · 80+ bitiruvchi" trust cue, mentor photo.
3. **Stats bar** — `10+ tizimlashtirilgan biznes · 80+ bitiruvchi · 40+ sotuv bo'limi zapuski · 7 yo'nalish`
4. **Audience split** — 2 cards: **Tadbirkorlar** ("Hodimlarni to'g'ri boshqarish") / **Top-menejerlar** ("Sotuvchilarni nazorat qilish")
5. **Before → After** — "Dasturdan avval" vs "2,5 oydan keyin"
6. **3 skills** — Raqamlarda boshqaruv · O'zgarishni boshqarish · O'sish nuqtalari
7. **Outcomes** — one bold statement block
8. **Program ("Dastur ichida")** — cards: 13 ta offline dars · Basyra AI (AI kurator) · Tadbiq menejer · Sotuv jamoasi onbordingi · 2 mehmon spiker · Online darslar
9. **Mentor** — large portrait + quote ("Men sizga motivatsiya emas — ishlaydigan tizim beraman") + signature "Abdulboriy"
10. **Cases** — logo/chip wall: Cambridge, Najot Ta'lim, K-Davo, Ucan Consulting, Basyra, edfix… + industries
11. **FAQ** (5 questions)
12. **Final CTA + application form** (Ism · Telefon · Biznes · Rol)
13. **Footer**

## 5. Universal rules (all variations)
- **Readable fonts only** — large, high legibility. No thin/condensed-illegible display at small sizes.
- **Generous whitespace**, clear hierarchy, big confident headlines.
- **ONE dominant accent color** per variation (+ at most one secondary pop).
- **Warm neutrals**, never pure #000 / #fff.
- Fully **responsive** (show desktop + mobile frames).
- Accessible contrast (≥ 4.5:1 body text).
- Rounded pill buttons; tasteful cards; subtle depth/shadows; light film-grain ok.

## VARIATION A — "WARM AUTHORITY" (premium dark)
*Reference feel: Justin Welsh × Marie Forleo, warm and expensive.*
- **Mood:** premium, calm, trustworthy, warm-cinematic.
- **Palette:** background warm near-black `#0E0B08` / `#15110B`; text cream `#F5EFE3`; **primary accent gold `#E8B873`**; secondary warm amber pop `#FF8A3D`; muted `#A89D89`.
- **Typography:** display = bold clean grotesk **Sora** (or similar) OR elegant serif **Playfair Display**; body = **Inter**. Big headlines, tight tracking.
- **Hero:** **full-bleed** cinematic photo of the mentor (warm golden side-light on a dark background); huge headline + gold accent word overlaid bottom-left; pill CTA in gold; small floating "result" cards over the photo (e.g. "+38% konversiya", "13 dars").
- **Imagery treatment:** warm, golden-hour, dark cinematic, slight grain.
- **Components:** glassy cards with faint gold borders, soft radial gold glow, rounded pills, film-grain texture, gold underline accents on key words.

- **Typography:** display = **bold condensed UPPERCASE** (Anton / Bebas Neue) for big punchy headlines; body = **Inter**.
- **Hero:** full-bleed dynamic lifestyle photo of the mentor (confident, mid-gesture or talking); oversized condensed all-caps headline; amber CTA; below it a row of **audience-segment photo cards** ("Tadbirkorlar uchun" / "Top-menejerlar uchun") each with a photo + "Batafsil →".
- **Imagery treatment:** dynamic, contrasty, real lifestyle/event photography; could use a subtle navy duotone on secondary images.
- **Components:** photo-topped cards, bold numbers, amber pills, strong section contrast, big animated-looking stats.

## VARIATION C — "WARM EDITORIAL" (bright & human)
*Reference feel: James Clear × Ali Abdaal — approachable premium.*
- **Mood:** approachable, human, premium-but-friendly.
- **Palette:** warm cream `#F6EFE2` / `#F0E4D2`; warm ink `#231B11`; **primary accent terracotta `#C66A38`**; secondary teal pop `#3E988A`.

- **Hero:** big **warm portrait of the mentor** beside a large friendly serif headline (split layout); soft shadows; terracotta CTA; relaxed, light, airy.
- **Imagery treatment:** bright, natural daylight, warm, friendly, real environments.
- **Components:** rounded cards with soft warm shadows, pill chips, gentle dividers, two cheerful accents used sparingly.

**Paste-ready prompt:**

## 6. Do / Don't
**Do:** real photography of the mentor front-and-center · big readable type · one strong accent · warm neutrals · generous spacing · clear single CTA repeated · Uzbek copy.
**Don't:** grey placeholder boxes · thin/illegible fonts · pure black/white · rainbow of colors · cramped layouts · stocky generic "corporate handshake" photos · loud infobiznes gradients.

## 7. Deliverable from the tool
For EACH of A / B / C: a full-page desktop homepage mockup + a mobile version, using the section list (§4) and the real Uzbek copy, in that variation's exact palette, typography, and imagery treatment.
