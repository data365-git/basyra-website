# Creative Concept — "SOTUV DETEKTIVI" (The Sales Detective)
### Business Navigator · Abdulboriy Abduqodirov — a Sherlock Holmes–themed website where every block is its own scene.

## THE BIG IDEA
Abdulboriy isn't a motivational coach — he's a **consulting detective for sales**. Every business is **an unsolved case**: sales aren't growing and nobody knows why. He **observes the evidence (raqamlar), follows the clues (where customers are lost), deduces the method, and reveals the SYSTEM** that closes the case. The whole site is styled like a **detective's case file / Victorian investigation study** — warm, dark, brass, parchment, a red investigation thread connecting everything.

**Spine line:** *"Har bir biznes — yechilishi kerak bo'lgan ish."* (Every business is a case to be solved.)
**Method line:** *Kuzatish → Tahlil → Xulosa → Tizim* (Observe → Analyze → Deduce → System)

## WHY IT FITS
- "Sotuvdagi eng katta muammo — odamlar emas, **tizimning yo'qligi**" = a deduction.
- O'sish nuqtalari (growth points) = **the clue** (where the customer "escapes").
- Raqamlar/KPI = **the evidence**.
- Basyra AI = the **digital Watson** — your assistant on the case.
- Tadbiq menejer = the **field agent** who works the case with you.

## VISUAL SYSTEM (evolves the warm-dark/gold look you liked — doesn't reset it)
- **Palette:** warm near-black `#0E0B08` · brass/gold `#E8B873` · parchment `#EAD9B0` · **oxblood-red investigation thread `#9B2D20`** (the new creative pop) · cream `#F5EFE3`.
- **Type:** elegant serif for "case dossier" headlines (Playfair / Cormorant feel) + clean sans (Inter) for body + a **typewriter/mono** for evidence tags & case numbers.
- **Recurring motifs (thread them across blocks):** magnifying glass, red string connecting clues, cork investigation board, wax-seal stamps ("ISH YOPILDI" / SOLVED), case-file folders, fingerprint, footprints/trail, brass desk lamp glow, handwritten chalk/ink annotations, "EVIDENCE / DALIL" tags, 221B-style door numbering.
- **Key rule:** each section is a DIFFERENT scene/object, not a repeated card. Variety is the point.

## BLOCK-BY-BLOCK (each individually art-directed)
1. **Nav** — brass plate logo + a small magnifying-glass mark; links as case-file tabs.
2. **HERO — "The Detective"** — Abdulboriy in a warm, lamplit study (leather, books, brass), headline "Sotuvlaringizni byudjet emas — *tizim* oshiradi"; floating **evidence tags** (+38%, 13 dars) pinned like clues; a faint red thread leads the eye down. Sub: "Har bir biznes — bir ish."
3. **Stats — "Case Record"** — numbers stamped like a dossier record sheet (10+ / 80+ / 40+ / 7), typewriter labels, a wax seal.
4. **Audience — "Two Client Files"** — Tadbirkor / Top-menejer as two **manila case folders** with a tab, a photo paperclipped, handwritten label.
5. **Before/After — THE INVESTIGATION BOARD (signature moment)** — "Oldin": a chaotic cork board, photos pinned, **tangled red threads**, question marks, messy = the unsolved case. "Keyin": the SAME board solved — clean straight red lines connecting clues into a rising arrow, a "ISH YOPILDI" wax stamp. This is the hero of the concept.
6. **Method — "The Deduction" (skills)** — 3 detective techniques, each its own object: **Kuzatish** (magnifying glass over numbers) · **Tahlil/O'zgarish** (footprint trail / connected dots) · **Xulosa/O'sish nuqtasi** (the clue circled in red where the customer escapes).
7. **Dastur tarkibi — "The Case Kit"** — the detective's desk drawer of tools: 13 offline dars (case sessions), Basyra AI ("raqamli Vatson"), Tadbiq menejer (field agent), jamoa onbordingi, 2 mehmon spiker, online darslar — each as a distinct labeled instrument, not a uniform card.
8. **Mentor — "The Consulting Detective"** — Abdulboriy portrait in the study, the quote as his signature deduction, ink signature.
9. **Keyslar — "Solved Cases / Yopilgan ishlar"** — each business (Cambridge, Najot Ta'lim, K-Davo, Ucan, Basyra, edfix…) as a **closed case file** with a red "TIZIMLASHTIRILDI" stamp; industries as evidence tags.
10. **FAQ — "The Questioning"** — open case questions.
11. **CTA — "Ishingizni oching"** (Open your case) — application as "submit the case."
12. **Footer** — brass nameplate, 221B-style address (Toshkent), red thread tying off.
